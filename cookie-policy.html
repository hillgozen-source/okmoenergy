Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how OKMO Power uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on https://okmopower.com.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files stored on your browser or device. They help websites remember preferences, measure performance and support advertising features.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Essential cookies for basic website operation
  • Analytics cookies to understand site traffic and performance
  • Preference cookies to remember settings where applicable
  • Advertising cookies or pixels to measure and optimize campaigns

3. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party providers such as Google, Meta/Facebook or analytics vendors may set cookies or pixels through this website. These providers control their own technologies and policies.

4. Google and Meta Advertising

We may use Google Ads, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar tools to measure ad performance, improve campaigns and show relevant advertising.

5. Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features or attribution tracking.

6. Interest-Based Advertising Choices

Users in the United States can learn more about ad choices at http://www.aboutads.info/choices. Users in the European Union can visit https://www.youronlinechoices.eu.
