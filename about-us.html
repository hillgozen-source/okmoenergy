About OKMO Power

Practical lithium power for RV, solar, marine and off-grid users.

Who We Are

OKMO Power is a product information and advertising website for OKMO lithium battery products. The site is operated by Shenzhen Maifeng Electronic Commerce Co., Ltd. (深圳市迈丰电子商务有限公司).

Our goal is to help shoppers understand OKMO LiFePO4 batteries before continuing to OKMOTech.com for current product details, pricing, availability, checkout and support.

What We Cover

We focus on batteries and power solutions for RVs, vans, solar energy storage, marine boats, trolling motors, golf carts and backup power systems.

Relationship With OKMOTech

Buttons and product links on this website direct visitors to OKMOTech.com. We do not process orders directly on this landing page.
